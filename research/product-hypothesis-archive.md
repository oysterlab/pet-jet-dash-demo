# Product Hypothesis Archive

Date: 2026-05-03

## Concept

Pet Jet Dash: upload a pet photo, transform it into a cute playable sticker/avatar, then play a short hold-to-fly challenge built for sharing.

## Current Product Direction

- Primary user: pet owners who already share pet photos on Instagram, TikTok, KakaoTalk, or similar social channels.
- Core promise: "Turn your pet into a playable sticker game in seconds."
- Game role: provide a playful context and shareable moment, not the main monetized product.
- Monetized object: the personalized pet character asset, style pack, export, or shareable GIF/video.

## Recommended Initial Game Loop

- 30-second Jetpack Joyride-style hold-to-fly challenge.
- Player presses/holds to rise and releases to fall.
- Collect snacks to increase combo.
- Near-miss obstacle dodges award bonus points.
- Boost gives short invulnerability and spectacle.
- Results should be shareable as image/GIF/video.

## Initial Art Direction

Primary style: Kawaii Sticker Pet.

Characteristics:
- Preserve recognizable pet face, markings, and personality.
- Simplify body into a rounded readable mascot.
- Use thick outlines and transparent background.
- Generate 3 core expressions: normal, excited, surprised.
- Add optional accessory such as goggles, scarf, tiny jetpack, crown, or snack bag.
- Ensure readability at 128-256px in gameplay.

## Initial Monetization Hypothesis

- Free: one basic pet character generation and playable game.
- $0.99: unlock one premium style/export pack.
- Strongest first paid pack: Toy Figure Pack or Kawaii Sticker Pack.
- Payment trigger: "save/share my pet as a premium playable sticker," not "unlock game mechanics."

## Open Questions

- Is the best buyer a casual pet owner, creator/pet influencer, memorial/gift buyer, or game-adjacent user?
- Is $0.99 too low relative to image generation cost and payment fees?
- Should the first paid action be one-time export, style unlock, or credits?
- Which visual style has the highest share and purchase intent among target users?
